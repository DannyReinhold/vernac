## Visiting this website

This static website is hosted using **GitHub Pages**. Delivering a page necessarily
transmits technical connection data to the hosting infrastructure, including your IP
address and request information such as the requested URL, time, browser information
and, where sent by your browser, the referring page. GitHub states that it records
visitors' IP addresses for security purposes, including visits made without a GitHub
account.

We use hosting to make the project documentation available and to maintain reliable,
secure operation. The legal basis for our processing is Article 6(1)(f) GDPR: our
legitimate interest in providing and securing this public information service.
Providing connection data is necessary for delivery; without it the website cannot
be retrieved. We do not use these visits to make automated decisions about you.

GitHub's privacy statement identifies GitHub, Inc. and GitHub B.V. as controllers
for processing within its scope. Its infrastructure and service providers may
process data outside the European Economic Area, including in the United States.
GitHub describes its transfer safeguards, including the EU–US Data Privacy Framework
where applicable and standard contractual clauses, in its privacy statement.
We do not set GitHub's security-log retention period. GitHub describes retention
by reference to the purpose of processing, security and legal obligations; no fixed
site-specific deletion period is represented here.

- [GitHub privacy statement, transfer safeguards and retention](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement)

## Cookies, local storage and external resources

The website code does not set cookies, use browser local storage, run analytics or
advertising trackers, or embed video, social feeds or externally hosted fonts.
Styles and images are served from this website. We do not request consent for
tracking because we do not implement such tracking. Normal delivery and browser
caching of requested pages remain necessary technical functions.

Links to GitHub, JetBrains and other websites do not embed those services. If you
follow a link, the destination provider processes the resulting request under its
own privacy terms. Our pages set a no-referrer policy. This policy does not prevent
a destination from receiving your IP address when you visit it.

## Contacting us

If you email or call us, we process your contact details and the content you provide
to answer your enquiry. Depending on the enquiry, the legal basis is Article 6(1)(b)
GDPR (steps towards or performance of a contract) or Article 6(1)(f) GDPR (handling
project correspondence). Communication service providers process messages as needed
for delivery. We retain correspondence while needed to handle the enquiry and any
related follow-up or legal claims. Applicable statutory retention obligations take
precedence; information no longer required is deleted. Do not send passwords, access
tokens or sensitive production data.

## Public contributions

Issues, discussions and pull requests on GitHub are public unless the interface
expressly states otherwise. Your account identity and contribution can be visible
and retained with the project history. Participation is voluntary. Our processing
for collaboration and maintenance is based on Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. GitHub's own
processing is described in its privacy statement. Please avoid publishing personal
information that is unnecessary for the technical discussion.

## Your rights

Subject to the applicable legal conditions, you may request access, rectification,
erasure, restriction of processing and data portability. **You may object to
processing based on Article 6(1)(f) GDPR on grounds relating to your particular
situation.** Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw it for the future;
this does not affect earlier lawful processing. Contact us using the details above.
For processing controlled independently by GitHub, you can also contact GitHub.

You may lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, particularly
in your place of habitual residence, work or the alleged infringement. The authority
for Bremen is the [Landesbeauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit Bremen](https://www.datenschutz.bremen.de/).

## Changes

We will update this notice if the website's services or processing change. Adding
