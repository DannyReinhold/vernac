# Website legal and privacy maintenance

The provider details were taken from the owner's Billvero legal notice on 11 October
2026, at his request. This is an implementation record, not a legal opinion or a
certification of compliance. Confirm that the address, VAT ID, contact details and
consumer-dispute statement remain applicable to Vernac before publishing.

The custom Jekyll layout applies to every documentation page. It replaces the
implicit GitHub Pages theme, including its external cdnjs anchor script. No consent
manager is installed: the authored site uses no optional cookies, local storage,
tracking, external fonts or embeds. A consent banner would not replace the duty to
provide a privacy notice for hosting and correspondence.

Before publishing and whenever hosting changes:

1. Build Pages and check legal links on the home page and a nested tutorial.
2. Inspect the deployed browser network/storage panels for injected resources or
   cookies. Repository inspection cannot establish DNS proxy or hosting settings.
3. Confirm GitHub's contractual role and any applicable data-processing agreement,
   international-transfer arrangements and retention information for the actual
   account. A public privacy notice cannot establish those arrangements for you.
4. Review email-provider arrangements, statutory retention duties and contact details.
5. Obtain legal review if additional business activities, regulated services,
   analytics, accounts or embedded content are introduced.

The Billvero source notice still used the former TMG reference and the discontinued
EU ODR platform. Those passages were not copied. Billvero itself was not changed.

For comparison, the German open-source organisation HedgeDoc e.V. publishes a
[provider notice](https://ev.hedgedoc.org/en/impressum/). OSS licensing does not by
itself determine whether a particular project presence needs a legal notice.

Implementation checks cover the repository layout and links. The deployed Pages
rendering, hosting account configuration and live browser storage still need review
after deployment. No claim of a completed legal audit is made.
