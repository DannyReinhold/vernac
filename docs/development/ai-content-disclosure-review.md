# AI content disclosure review for vernac.org

Status: **queued; first follow-up after completion of the debugger workstream**.
No legal assessment or conclusion has been made in this planning record. Do not
run this investigation concurrently with the current debugger implementation.

## Question to resolve

Vernac.org and the public Vernac project contain AI-assisted or AI-generated prose,
illustrations and code, with human review and editing. Determine whether German or
EU requirements impose disclosure, marking or other transparency duties on this
specific publication and its operator, and what concrete action is required.

## Investigation scope, when this work starts

- Establish the actual publication workflow and distinguish assisted drafting,
  reviewed/edited content and any fully automated publication.
- Check applicability separately for public documentation, images/illustrations,
  source code and generated application code. Do not assume all categories have
  the same rules, or that every use of an AI tool creates the same obligation.
- Verify the relevant German/EU rules against current primary official sources,
  including scope, exceptions, responsible roles and effective/application dates.
  Distinguish binding duties from voluntary transparency recommendations.
- Check whether the project's existing imprint/privacy pages or another suitable
  place need a change; determine content-specific marking needs separately.
- Produce a dated assessment with sources, unresolved factual questions and concrete
  proposed website/repository changes. Obtain qualified legal review where needed
  rather than presenting an automated assessment as a guarantee of compliance.

## Expected deliverable

A concise, source-backed assessment for the actual Vernac setup and, where indicated,
a reviewable patch for the appropriate English website/documentation notices or
markings. Avoid blanket statements that misrepresent how individual content was
created. This work does not itself change Vernac's project or extension licensing.
