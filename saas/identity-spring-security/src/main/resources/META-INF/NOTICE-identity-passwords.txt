Modifications by the Vernac project (2026): Unicode NFC normalization; retain
15-128-code-point entries within the credential/line-format bounds; remove
duplicates; deterministic UTF-8 sorting and LF output. The corpus, manifest and
curation statistics are in org/vernac/saas/identity/security/credentials/passwords.
The original frequency ranking is not retained. No account associations are used.

This is a finite common-password screening baseline, not the complete HIBP corpus
or a guarantee that an accepted password has never appeared in a breach. The pinned
commit does not establish the freshness of the underlying historical source data.
No runtime network update or transmission of submitted passwords is performed.
